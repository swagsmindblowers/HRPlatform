# Customer terms

**Needed before:** public launch.
**Status:** not started — stub only.

## What this needs to cover
- A no-advice clause matching the wording standard in [advice-boundary-policy.md](./advice-boundary-policy.md) — the product is a cost-modelling and comparison tool, never immigration, tax, or legal advice.
- A liability cap appropriate to a cost-estimation tool whose figures a customer may rely on when making a hiring decision.
- Acceptable use terms covering the public calculator (no login) and the in-app product separately, since they have different data-collection footprints.

## What's needed from a professional
A commercial lawyer to draft terms of service before the public calculator or BetterOff HR integration is opened to real customers.
